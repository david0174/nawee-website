4. Contact by Email or Telephone

If you contact us by email or telephone, we store and process your information solely for the purpose of handling your inquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1) (b) or (f) GDPR. The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer required for this purpose.

Emails to us are received, stored and sent via the email service Zoho Mail provided by Zoho Corporation B.V. (Netherlands). In particular, your name, email address, the content of your message and technical email data are processed. The data is processed in a Zoho data center in the EU. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in reliable email communication).

Online Withdrawal via Zoho Forms

For the electronic withdrawal from contracts (“Withdraw from contract”), we use a form provided by the service Zoho Forms of Zoho Corporation B.V. (Netherlands). The form is only loaded on the “Withdraw from contract” page. Zoho processes your name, email address, the details of the contract withdrawn from, your withdrawal statement, the time of receipt and technical data such as your IP address. The data is processed in a Zoho data center in the EU.

We use this data to process your withdrawal and to confirm its receipt. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with § 356a of the German Civil Code (BGB) (statutory obligation to provide an electronic withdrawal function) and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Further information can be found in Zoho’s privacy policy.

5. Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we process your email address to send you tips, insights and ideas on English, AI and digital skills. Registration uses a double opt-in procedure: you first receive an email containing a confirmation link. Only after your confirmation will we add you to the mailing list. We store the date and time of registration and confirmation, as well as your IP address, to demonstrate that consent was obtained.

The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, for example via the unsubscribe link in each email or by contacting us at david@nawee.net. The lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal remains unaffected.

The newsletter is sent via Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, 106 boulevard Haussmann, 75008 Paris, France). We have a data processing agreement with Brevo. The subscription form is part of our own website; no content from Brevo is loaded when you simply visit the page. Only when you click “Subscribe” are your email address and IP address transmitted to Brevo.

7. Appointment Booking (Cal.com) and Payment (Stripe)

To book appointments (e.g. the free initial consultation), we use the service Cal.com provided by Cal.com, Inc. When you click a booking link, you are redirected to Cal.com's booking page. No Cal.com content is loaded on our website itself.

When you make a booking, Cal.com processes the data you enter, in particular your name, email address, phone number and message where provided, and the appointment you choose. We use this data to hold the appointment and to contact you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures or performance of a contract).

Booked appointments are automatically transferred to our Google Calendar so that we can manage availability and avoid double bookings. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Your name, email address and appointment details are transmitted to Google. A transfer to the USA is possible; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Where data is transferred to the USA, this takes place on the basis of appropriate safeguards in accordance with Art. 44 et seq. GDPR. Further information can be found in Cal.com's Privacy Policy .

10. Storage Period

We only store personal data for as long as is necessary for the respective purpose:

  • Bookings and customer data: for the duration of our work together. Invoices and tax-relevant documents are then kept for as long as required by law (in particular under § 147 of the German Fiscal Code (AO), usually eight to ten years).
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. We keep proof of your consent for as long as we need it to defend against possible claims.
  • Server log files: deleted by the hosting provider according to its own retention periods; we do not have access to them.
