NAWEE AS AN APP

Nawee on your phone

Install Nawee on your home screen – free and without an app store. Then you open Nawee with one tap, in full screen, and pages you have already opened also work without internet.

✅ You’re already using Nawee as an app – great!

  • 👆One tap – and you’re learning
  • 📱Full screen, without the browser bar
  • ✈️Offline: lessons and picture cards you visited also work without internet
  • 🆓Free, no sign-up, no app store

How to install Nawee

🍎 iPhone & iPad

  1. Open nawee.net in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⬆︎ (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  4. Tap “Add” – done!

🤖 Android

  1. Open nawee.net in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ (three dots) at the top right.
  3. Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  4. Confirm with “Install” – done!

💻 Computer

  1. Open nawee.net in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon ⊕ in the address bar.
  3. Confirm with “Install”.

💡 Tip: Log in to the app with your free account. On the iPhone, the app saves your progress separately from the browser – with an account it’s the same everywhere.
