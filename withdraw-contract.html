Withdraw from contract

Would you like to withdraw from a contract concluded online with Nawee? You can submit your withdrawal electronically using the form below.

Please provide your name, the details of the contract concerned and your withdrawal statement. You do not need to give a reason for your withdrawal.

The form is in German. Vorname / Nachname = first / last name, Welchen Vertrag möchten Sie widerrufen? = which contract, Datum des Vertragsschlusses = date of the contract, Widerrufserklärung = your withdrawal statement.

After submitting, you will receive a confirmation that your withdrawal has been received.

You can also declare your withdrawal in other ways, e.g. by email to david@nawee.net. Further information can be found in the withdrawal information.
