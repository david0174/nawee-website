ABOUT ME

Personal. Practical. Future-focused.

I’m David Nawee. At Nawee, I bring together my experience in language, programming and learning.

I studied Philosophy, Politics and Law at Binghamton University in New York. My studies gave me a broad understanding of different social, political and legal systems and trained me to understand and analyze complex structures.

I then completed an intensive six-month program in programming and computer science at the NYU Tandon School of Engineering, with a focus on the fundamentals of computer science and C++. I later deepened this knowledge through my own work as a web developer.

For many years, I have also worked as a math tutor. This taught me to understand different people and their individual learning needs, and to prepare them specifically for their goals.

Today, these experiences form the foundation of my work at Nawee: teaching English and AI – including programming with AI – in a clear and understandable way, and helping people apply knowledge independently.
