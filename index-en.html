HOW YOU LEARN AT NAWEE

Listen first, then speak

The way children learn their first language: listen, understand, repeat – and then speak yourself. Reading and writing help, but they’re not the focus.

  1. 1

    Listen

    Real conversations, spoken by a native speaker. You only read the text if you want to.

  2. 2

    Understand

    Short questions show you whether you caught the key points.

  3. 3

    Repeat

    Say it after me, record yourself and compare. Your recording stays on your device.

  4. 4

    Speak

    Answer out loud, practice in role-plays and take a mission into your everyday life.
