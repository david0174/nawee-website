ABOUT ME

Progress over perfection.Confidence through practice.

I’m David Nawee, English and communication coach – born and raised in the US. For me, learning a language is about more than knowing the right words. It’s about having the confidence to express yourself and take part in conversations without being held back by the fear of making mistakes. That’s the philosophy behind Nawee.

In my personalized English training and communication coaching, we focus on practical speaking, constructive feedback, and applying what you learn to real-life situations, at work and in everyday conversations. My experience as a certified life coach helps me understand what holds people back, and how to build confidence step by step.

You don’t need to be perfect before you start speaking. I know this from experience: I learned German as an adult and reached C1 in less than three years – making plenty of mistakes along the way. Mistakes are part of learning, but we don’t stop there: with targeted feedback, we work on eliminating your typical mistakes so you speak English accurately and with confidence.
